01 / Who we are

Our community is stronger
when we show up.

Since 2023, OSI has brought people together to serve Oakland County.

Our work brings education, community service, and medical volunteering together. From food assistance and planting to the Rochester Heritage Festival and micro:bit learning, each is a way to care for the place we call home.

Our team includes three Eagle Scout candidates.
