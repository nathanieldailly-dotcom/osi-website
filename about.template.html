Why we started / 2023

Care should lead
to action.

In 2023, we were frustrated that too few students were taking an active role in the health of Oakland County and their own communities.

Oakland Service Initiative began as a way to turn that concern into action: bringing people together to learn, volunteer, and care for the place we call home.
